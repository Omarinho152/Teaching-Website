À propos

01 / ENSEIGNANT

Bonjour, je m'appelle Omar. French is my first language — I've lived in Belgium since 2008, so it's not just a language I studied, it's the one I think, argue and joke in.

Before turning to French teaching, I spent several years teaching maths and physics at GCSE and A-level, so lesson planning, pacing and explaining things three different ways until one clicks is already second nature. I bring that same structure to French: clear levels, clear goals, and homework that's actually useful.

I focus on getting students speaking early rather than drilling grammar in isolation, while still being rigorous about the fundamentals — verb conjugation, gender, the sounds that don't exist in English — that trip most learners up.
